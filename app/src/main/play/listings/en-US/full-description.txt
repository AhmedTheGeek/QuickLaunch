Quick Launch is the fastest way to open an app on Android. Type two or three letters, press Enter, and you're there. No home screen pages, no folders, no scrolling through the app drawer.

It's a launcher overlay, not a home screen replacement. Keep the launcher you have. Quick Launch is a small search card that appears over whatever you're doing and disappears the moment you launch something.

WHY PEOPLE SWITCH
• Any app is two letters away. Results update on every keystroke, ranked so the app you mean comes first.
• It speaks your shorthand. Prefixes, word starts and initials all match: "yt" opens YouTube, "gm" opens Gmail.
• It knows what you'll open next. The apps you use most rise to the top, and with Usage access they're listed before you type at all.
• Never leave the keyboard. Enter opens, Up and Down move, Esc closes. Tab, Ctrl+N and Ctrl+P work too.
• Summon it anywhere. Instant mode draws the card within a frame of the trigger, over any app.
• Drag to split screen. Long-press a result and drop it beside the app you're in.
• Work profile apps are included and badged.

FAST IS THE FEATURE
• Search runs in under a millisecond per keystroke. No spinners, ever.
• Tiny download, no background service, no network access.
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts.

MADE FOR PEOPLE WHO TYPE
• Physical keyboards, Samsung DeX, foldables and tablets: the card is centred and sized for large screens.
• Ctrl+Space opens Quick Launch from any app when a keyboard is attached.
• Or bind it to the side key, a gesture, or Good Lock One Hand Operation+.

PERMISSIONS, PLAINLY
• Display over other apps (optional): instant mode.
• Usage access (optional): "most used" ordering. Read on your device, never sent anywhere.
• Accessibility service (optional): only to hear Ctrl+Space. It reads no screen content.

Everything stays on your device. Free and open source (GPL-3.0): github.com/AhmedTheGeek/QuickLaunch
